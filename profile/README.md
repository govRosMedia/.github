## ℹ️ About

**Ros.Media** ([ros.media](https://ros.media)) is a social-media marketing and promotion service — grow your audience across social networks, complete and earn from CPA tasks, and integrate payments into your bots and sites.

## 📦 Open-source
- **[telegram-stars-cpa-tasks-bot](https://github.com/govRosMedia/telegram-stars-cpa-tasks-bot)** — CPA task exchange bot (earn Telegram Stars & TON for tasks), with referrals & partner API
- **[SMOFast-Job-and-order-management](https://github.com/govRosMedia/SMOFast-Job-and-order-management)** — task/order management (smofast.com)
- **[SMM-App-WebViewSample-WebViewApp-Android](https://github.com/govRosMedia/SMM-App-WebViewSample-WebViewApp-Android)** — Android WebView app sample
